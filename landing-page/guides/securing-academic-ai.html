Securing Academic Research Intelligence

Protecting high-authority research tools like Consensus AI and Elicit from large-scale data harvesting.

Academic AI tools such as Consensus AI, Elicit AI, and Gauth AI manage vast amounts of proprietary and structured research data. Because this data is highly valuable for training competitive LLMs, these platforms are constant targets for sophisticated "harvesting" bots.

The Research Extraction Problem

Bot operators use Math AI and Question AI patterns to automate the extraction of complex research papers and citation graphs. This not only steals intellectual property but also strains the search infrastructure of these specialized platforms.

Sentinel's High-Authority Analysis

Sentinel helps academic platforms maintain their competitive edge:

  • Credential Stuffing Prevention: Prevent botnets from using stolen credentials to access premium research databases.
  • Semantic Scraper Blocking: Identify requests that follow a "systematic crawl" pattern, even when disguised as legitimate user queries.
  • Regional Governance: Block scraping attempts from high-risk infrastructure nodes known for data harvesting.
