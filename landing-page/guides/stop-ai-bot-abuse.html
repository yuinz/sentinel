Stopping the Surge of AI Bot Abuse

How to defend your API endpoints against the next generation of automated threats like Agentic AI and high-velocity scrapers.

In 2026, the landscape of bot traffic has shifted. Traditional IP blacklisting is no longer enough to stop Agentic AI tools that can rotate through thousands of residential IPs in seconds. Stop AI bot abuse by moving from reactive blocking to proactive trust synthesis.

Why Traditional AI Detectors Fail

Most "AI detector" tools look for patterns in text or static request headers. Advanced bots now mimic Claude AI or Perplexity AI signatures to blend in with legitimate developer traffic. Sentinel bypasses this cat-and-mouse game by analyzing infrastructure forensics.

The Sentinel Strategy: Behavioral Trust

  • ASN Governance: Instantly identify if a request is coming from a datacenter range commonly used by malicious Gemini AI scrapers.
  • Velocity Entropy: Track patterns that exceed human capability, even when spread across multiple IPs.
  • BWT Puzzles: Force automated tools to solve cryptographic work, increasing their operational cost until they quit.
